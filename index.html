Import bank statement

Load an Excel (.xlsx/.xls) or CSV export from your bank. Review the transactions and classifications before adding anything to the Expense Ledger. Your file is parsed inside your browser; the app does not upload your statement to GitHub. For Excel files, the XLSX parser is loaded from cdnjs when needed; CSV import works without it.
Select your bank statement to begin.
Important: Only outgoing transactions are imported into the spending ledger. Salary credits, refunds and transfers between accounts need separate review. Credit card bill payments should be classified as Transfer when their purchases are already recorded. The latest statement balance is a dated cash snapshot, not your available discretionary budget.

Review before import

Uncheck any item you do not want imported. Change its type to Fixed, Debt, Savings or Transfer if it should not reduce your daily discretionary allowance.

Debt Center

Budget repayments by payday cycle (20th–19th), track actual payments and see how card purchases change outstanding balances. Enter your intended loan amount as a reference, not as an outstanding balance, until disbursement is confirmed.
Balances reflect your stated opening balance plus linked ledger movements after its opening date. Unknown opening balances are never assumed to be zero. Card purchases count as spending once; regular card settlements are cash transfers, not new spending. To reduce your daily spending budget for older card debt, enter an additional payoff target (up to your planned card payment).

Add or edit a debt account

To account for a new loan disbursement, enter a Loan disbursement movement below. A loan application amount alone does not create a debt.

Record a debt movement

Loan repayments reduce principal by payment minus interest (entered manually; interest is not accrued automatically). Card payments reduce the outstanding balance but do not reduce the discretionary budget again. Card purchases are entered in Expense Ledger with payment method Credit card.

Rollover preferences

Automatic rollover from imported historical transactions is disabled. Previous months carry forward only after you explicitly approve a reviewed cycle.
The remainder goes to savings. Default: 50% to spending, 50% to savings. Deficits are flagged, not carried forward automatically.
Rollover is locked on explicit closeout. Changes to a closed period invalidate approval and require rechecking. Imports do not automatically create forward balances.

Your data & backups

Data is stored locally in this browser. A standalone HTML file does not automatically sync across devices; clearing browser storage can delete it. Export backups regularly.
For long-term multi-device use, the same interface can later be hosted with a secure cloud database.
